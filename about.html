About QuakeSound

QuakeSound turns live earthquake data into a musical soundscape. Each event plays a layered tone.

  • Pitch follows magnitude, snapped to an A minor pentatonic so overlaps stay consonant.
  • Note length follows depth. Shallower events ring longer.
  • Intensity follows magnitude. Larger events sustain longer, fade slower, and sit a little louder.

The feed is the USGS Earthquake GeoJSON feed. The map is Leaflet on CARTO Dark Matter. Markers run from green to red by magnitude, and a ripple marks each note. Sound is synthesized in the browser: layered oscillators, a shaped reverb, and gain that eases off when many notes overlap.

Modes

  • Last hour — notes fire as events arrive.
  • Past day — the day’s quakes play in sequence and loop.

An earlier map and synth remains at /v1/. It is not the current site.
